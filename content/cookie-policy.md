## 1) What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. Cookies set by Optivads are called first-party cookies. Cookies set by third parties (e.g., Google, Meta, LinkedIn) are called third-party cookies.

Similar technologies (pixels, tags, SDKs, device IDs) may also be used.

## 2) Why do we use cookies?

We use cookies for several purposes:

* To make our site work securely and reliably (strictly necessary).
* To remember your preferences (functionality).
* To analyze traffic and improve services (analytics).
* To measure campaigns and show relevant ads (advertising/targeting).

Some cookies are essential and do not require consent. Others are only used if you consent via our Cookie Consent Manager.

## 3) Types of cookies we use

### a) Strictly Necessary / Essential

Always active. Required for core site functions.

**Examples:** session management, security, load balancing.

### b) Analytics & Performance

Measure how our site is used and where we can improve.

**Examples:** Google Analytics cookies (_ga, _gid), performance/error monitoring.

### c) Functionality

Remember choices (e.g., language, region, form preferences).

**Examples:** language preference cookies, form completion cookies.

### d) Advertising & Targeting

Track conversions, retarget visitors, measure ad performance.

**Examples:** Meta Pixel (_fbp), LinkedIn Insight Tag (li_sugr).

## 5) Managing cookies & your choices

You can manage your cookie preferences through our Cookie Consent Manager at any time.

### Consent/withdrawal

Non-essential cookies (analytics, advertising) run only if you opt in. You can withdraw consent at any time.

### Browser controls

Most browsers allow blocking/removing cookies (see [allaboutcookies.org](https://www.allaboutcookies.org)).

### Mobile devices

Use device privacy settings (e.g., IDFA/GAID reset, limit ad tracking).

### Third-party opt-outs

* [Google Analytics Opt-out](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout)
* [Meta/Facebook Ads Opt-out](https://www.facebook.com/help/568137493302217)
* [LinkedIn Ads Opt-out](https://www.linkedin.com/psettings/guest-controls/retargeting-opt-out)

Blocking cookies may affect site performance.
